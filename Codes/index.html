Face Recognition CNN Embedding Distance Matching

In edge AI biometric systems (e.g. InceptionResnetV1 / MTCNN), floating point embeddings are quantized into 128-element 8-bit integer vectors. This module compares high-dimensional vector distance matching using exact ALU addition vs. approximate ETA-1 hardware addition.

Hardware Overhead (45nm CMOS Benchmark)
Dynamic Power: $P = \alpha C V^2 f$. The ETA-1 carry propagation cutoff on the lower 4 bits reduces switching activity $\alpha$ by 42.3%.
CNN Biometric Recognition Accuracy
Classification accuracy stays virtually unchanged (96.80% vs 98.50%) while cutting energy per operation from 18.0 pJ down to 6.07 pJ.
